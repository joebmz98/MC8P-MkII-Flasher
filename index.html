MC8P MkII MIDI Controller

Official WebHID Firmware Flasher (HEX Engine)

Hardware: WeAct STM32F411 v3.0 Firmware: v3.1 (.hex)
Requirements: Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Connect USB-C while holding the KEY button to enter WeAct Bootloader mode.

Flashing Instructions

  1. Unplug USB-C cable.
  2. Press and hold the KEY button on the board.
  3. Plug in USB-C while holding KEY, then release. The LED will pulse smoothly.
  4. Click Flash Firmware and select WeAct STM32 HID Bootloader.
