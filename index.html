MC8P MkII MIDI Controller

Official WebUSB / HID Web Flashing Portal

Hardware: WeAct STM32F411 v3.0 Firmware: v3.1
Requirements: Must use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Opera on Desktop. Connect your board using a USB-C data cable directly (avoid unshielded charge-only cables).

Flashing Instructions

  1. Connect your WeAct Blackpill board to your computer via USB-C.
  2. Put board into HID Bootloader mode: press and hold BOOT0, press NRST once, then release BOOT0.
  3. Click the Flash Firmware button below.
  4. Select your STM32 HID / Serial device from the browser popup dialog and click Connect.
