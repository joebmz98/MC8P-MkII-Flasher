MC8P MkII MIDI Controller

Official WebUSB / DFU Flashing Portal

Hardware: WeAct STM32F411 v3.0 Firmware: v3.1
Requirements: Use Chrome or Edge. Ensure board is in DFU Mode (BOOT0 held during reset).

Flashing Instructions

  1. Connect board via USB-C.
  2. Put into DFU bootloader mode: Hold BOOT0, press NRST, then release BOOT0.
  3. Click Flash Firmware and select STM32 BOOTLOADER from the browser window.
