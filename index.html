MC8P MkII MIDI Controller

Official WebHID Firmware Flashing Portal

Hardware: WeAct STM32F411 v3.0 Firmware: v3.1
Requirements: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Hold down the KEY button while connecting USB-C to enter WeAct Bootloader mode.

Flashing Instructions

  1. Unplug USB-C cable.
  2. Press and hold the KEY button (User Button) on the board.
  3. Plug in USB-C while holding KEY, then release. The onboard LED will pulse smoothly.
  4. Click Flash Firmware and select WeAct STM32 HID Bootloader.
